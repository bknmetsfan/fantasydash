## Gotchas
- Sleeper's guillotine leagues DO assign `matchup_id`s (one per roster); pool detection
  uses `settings.type == 3` or all-unique ids — don't "fix" it back to `is None`.
- Sleeper keeps projecting players who are Out. Zeroing is in `load_projections()`;
  `INJ_OUT` decides which statuses count. Questionable is NOT zeroed.
- Degenerates has `disable_elimination` (manual chop) and skipped the Week 1 chop —
  lowest score ≠ chopped there. Paris/Red Queen's eliminate automatically.
- Chop-line sign: `margin` is negative when you ARE the lowest (gap to next team up).
  `rank` is 1 = top scorer; on-the-block is `rank == field_size`.
- ESPN play-by-play needs `User-Agent: curl/8.0` — browser UAs get Akamai 403.
- Feed shows only in-progress games; finished games stay until pruned by the scoreboard.
- `flyctl deploy` restarts the machine: in-memory caches reset (feed, plays, waivers);
  the SQLite log on `/data` persists. The preview server in the desktop app dies when
  the pane closes — irrelevant now that Fly hosts it.
- Symptom "offline" in the header = the page's `/api/state` fetch failed (server
  restarting or auth cookie missing).
